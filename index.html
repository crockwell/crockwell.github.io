Chris Rockwell

AI Scientist, Roblox

I am an AI Scientist working on large-scale generative models for photorealistic gaming at Roblox Reality. Previously, I was part of the founding team at Morpheus AI under Xun Huang, which was acquired by Roblox.

I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Michigan, advised by Justin Johnson and David F. Fouhey. I also interned at NVIDIA Deep Imagination Research with Chen-Hsuan Lin and Tsung-Yi Lin, and at Meta Reality Labs with Hung-Yu Tseng and Jia-Bin Huang.

Across these roles, I have worked on data, modeling, training and evaluation, from camera pose estimation and 3D generation to real-time video generation and world models.
